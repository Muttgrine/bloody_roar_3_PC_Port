===============================================
  DISC REQUIREMENTS
===============================================

For multiplayer, all players must use the same disc image.

Game:    Bloody Roar 3
Region:  USA (NTSC-U)
Serial:  SLUS-20212
CRC:     AA4E5A35

SHA-256: c2c505f1f639cc5774da7d17eea90571fe066ee538706c238cb76b04d13235a7

Format:  ISO (2048 bytes/sector)
         If you have a BIN/CUE, the launcher converts it automatically.

Place your disc image as:
  games\br3\disc.iso

To verify your image matches, open PowerShell and run:
  Get-FileHash games\br3\disc.iso -Algorithm SHA256

The hash must match the SHA-256 above for online play.
